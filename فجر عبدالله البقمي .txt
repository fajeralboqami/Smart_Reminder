# Smart Reminder Experiment on Injaz

Capstone project for Experimentation & Causal Inference — SDAIA Academy.

## Files

- `Capstone_Smart_Reminder_FINAL.ipynb` — completed and executed capstone notebook
- `injaz_users.csv` — synthetic Injaz user dataset used by the notebook

## Project

The project designs and evaluates a randomized experiment for a Smart Reminder feature on the fictional Injaz platform. It covers selection bias, randomization, power and sample-size planning, A/A testing, primary metric analysis, CUPED, sequential peeking, balance checks, subgroup analysis, and a final decision memo.

## 📌 Executive Summary
* **Final Recommendation:** **SHIP** the "Smart Reminder" feature to 100% of platform users.
* **Core Finding:** The randomized experiment confirmed a statistically significant **+12.26 percentage point** (+12.3% relative lift) increase in service completion rates.

---

## 📊 Key Results & Empirical Findings

| Metric | Control | Treatment | Lift / Impact | Statistical Significance |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Service Completion Rate** | Baseline ($Y_0$) | Treatment ($Y_1$) | **+12.26 pp** | $p < 0.05$ (Statistically Significant) |

### **Why Observational Data Failed (Selection Bias)**
* **Naive Estimate:** High-engagement users self-selected into using reminders, leading to an inflated observational estimate of **+16.20 pp**.
* **True Causal Impact:** The randomized A/B experiment isolated the true causal impact at **+12.26 pp** (Oracle True ATE: **+12.63 pp**).

---

## ⚙️ Experimental Design & Sample Size
* **Target Power:** $80\%$ ($\beta = 0.20$) at $\alpha = 0.05$.
* **Minimum Detectable Effect (MDE):** $10\%$ relative lift over baseline.
* **Required Sample Size:** Calculated per arm based on baseline conversion rate.
* **Runtime:** 5 days at an average daily traffic of 2,000 eligible users.

---

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendation & Next Steps
1. **Full Rollout:** Expand exposure from 50% to 100% across all mobile and web platforms.
2. **Targeted Optimization:** Focus future Copy/UX iterations on users with lower digital literacy scores, who showed the highest relative lift from automated reminders.
